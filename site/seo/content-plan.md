# Twelve-week search and enquiry plan

The keyword map is an editorial starting point based on existing services, not measured search-volume research. Check Search Console queries after launch before expanding into new pages. Improve the existing destination when a topic already has a useful page.

| Timing | Work | Destination / evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Verify search accounts, submit sitemap, check selected canonicals and claim the business listing. | SEO-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md; DNS and listing access. |
| Week 2 | Review how to identify the pump model and part when enquiring. | Improve Mining & Industrial / Models & Parts Sheet with company-approved examples; retain the 59 source pairings. |
| Weeks 3–4 | Explain excavation, formwork and casting for V-drainage, using actual site photographs and scope. | Improve Construction and its gallery. Get technical review and permission before naming a new client/site. |
| Weeks 5–6 | Answer common pump accessory questions: reference, material, dimensions and quantity. | Improve Pump Accessories; use approved specifications and the existing selected-part quotation path. |
| Weeks 7–8 | Explain how farms can brief a harvest team: fruit, location, dates, scale and tasks. | Improve Agriculture; reuse original gallery photographs with accurate captions. Avoid unsupported yields, team capacity and availability. |
| Weeks 9–10 | Add one well-supported construction case study or update an existing record. | Confirm status, dates, scope and approved client attribution. Keep bid amounts withheld. The Dwellers 2025 status is currently unconfirmed. |
| Weeks 11–12 | Review lifting and rigging product enquiries and search performance. | Improve Lifting & Rigging with reviewed product references. Do not add inspection/certification or load-rating claims without evidence. |

Each update should help the visitor decide what to enquire about and link to the relevant service or selected-product form. Use descriptive image alternatives and meaningful captions. Add new pages only when there is a distinct, substantive topic; avoid nearly identical pages for towns where the company has not established an actual presence.

Keep a monthly record of search clicks, non-brand queries, landing pages and actual qualified enquiries. Compare service pages individually. Review the website and Business Profile details whenever company contact information or services change.
